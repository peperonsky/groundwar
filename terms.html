What Groundwar is

Groundwar is a free, no-account tool that shows historical battles, events and notable people near a place you choose, pulled live from Wikidata and Wikipedia. It's provided for personal, informational and educational use.

No warranty on the data

Groundwar has no editorial staff and keeps no database of its own. Everything you see, including dates, locations, names and summaries, comes directly from Wikidata and Wikipedia at the moment you load the page. Those are open, crowd-edited sources: most entries are accurate, but some are incomplete, approximate, outdated or occasionally wrong. Don't rely on anything shown here for academic citation, legal, medical or financial decisions, or any other purpose where accuracy actually matters. Check the linked Wikipedia article, and its own sources, before treating anything as fact.
