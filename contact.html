Fixing the data yourself

Everything on Groundwar comes from Wikidata, which anyone can edit. If a battle is missing because Wikidata has no location for it, adding its coordinates there (the "coordinate location" property) puts it on Groundwar at the next weekly rebuild, and fixes it for every other site that uses Wikidata too. The About page explains where everything comes from.

What happens to your message

It's emailed to the site owner, with the page you sent it from and your browser type, and isn't stored anywhere else. Your email address is only used to reply. The privacy policy has the details.
